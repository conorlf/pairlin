IOSS RULES AND ELIGIBILITY — EU VAT ONE STOP SHOP (IMPORT)

IOSS (Import One Stop Shop) was introduced on 1 July 2021 under EU VAT Directive 2017/2455.

ELIGIBILITY
- Applies to goods imported from outside the EU with intrinsic value not exceeding EUR 150
- Sellers or marketplace facilitators must register in one EU member state
- Registration covers all B2C sales to EU consumers
- Available to EU-based sellers and non-EU sellers (via intermediary where required)

HOW IT WORKS
- Seller collects VAT at point of sale at the destination country rate
- Seller declares and remits VAT monthly via the IOSS portal
- Parcels arrive with IOSS VAT ID printed on customs declaration (CN22/CN23)
- Customs release parcels without additional VAT/duty collection at border
- Parcel value must be accurately declared — misrepresentation voids IOSS

JULY 2026 REFORM — €3 PER TARIFF HEADING
From 1 July 2026, EU reform introduces a €3 customs duty charge per distinct HS tariff heading
for IOSS-registered goods valued under €150. This replaces the previous zero-duty threshold.
- Each distinct 4-digit HS heading in a shipment attracts one €3 charge
- VAT continues to be collected at checkout by IOSS-registered sellers
- Multiple items from the same HS heading count as one €3 charge
- Mixed-category baskets (e.g. clothing + cosmetics + accessories) will incur multiple €3 charges

IOSS REGISTERED INDICATORS (for consumer-facing detection)
- VAT amount displayed and calculated at checkout
- "All taxes included" or "No additional charges on delivery" language
- EUR pricing with explicit VAT line item breakdown
- "Duties and taxes paid" language in terms and conditions
- Visible EU VAT registration number (format: EUxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx)
- Checkout confirmation showing VAT collected
- "Import VAT included" text anywhere on checkout page

NON-IOSS INDICATORS
- "Customs charges may apply on delivery"
- "Import duties are the buyer's responsibility"
- GBP pricing with no VAT mention for EU destinations
- Vague or absent customs policy
- No VAT registration number visible
- No VAT line item at checkout
- "Prices shown exclude VAT"
- "International shipping — taxes may apply"

THRESHOLD RULES
- Under EUR 150: IOSS applies if seller is registered; from July 2026 €3/heading duty still applies
- EUR 150 and above: IOSS does not apply regardless of registration status
- Full customs duty + import VAT charged at border for all goods over EUR 150
- The EUR 150 threshold is based on intrinsic value excluding transport and insurance

COMMON IOSS-REGISTERED RETAILERS (indicative, not exhaustive)
- Shein (registered, China-origin, high volume, spot checks reported)
- Temu (registered, China-origin)
- AliExpress (registered for marketplace sales)
- Amazon (registered as marketplace facilitator)
- ASOS (registered, UK-origin)
- Zara (EU-based, IOSS registered)

RETAILERS KNOWN NOT TO USE IOSS
- Gymshark (UK-based, not registered as of 2025)
- Boohoo Group (UK-based, not registered)
- Many small UK DTC brands post-Brexit
- US retailers without EU IOSS registration

SPOT CHECK RISK
Even IOSS-registered parcels can be held at customs due to:
- Database validation errors (IOSS number not found in customs system)
- Carrier paperwork errors (wrong IOSS number on label)
- Spot checks on high-volume retailers (Shein, Temu frequently targeted)
- Declared value mismatches
- Restricted or prohibited goods misclassification
