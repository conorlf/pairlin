AN POST CUSTOMS FEES — IRELAND
Source: An Post official fee schedule (2025-2026)

CUSTOMS CLEARANCE AND HANDLING FEES

Standard customs clearance fee: €3.50 per parcel
Handling fee: €2.50 per parcel
Total standard customs charge: €6.00 per parcel

This fee applies when An Post processes a parcel through Irish customs.
The fee is charged to the recipient regardless of parcel value.
An Post does not charge this fee if the sender has pre-paid customs (IOSS or DDP).

FROM JULY 2026 — IOSS REFORM
For IOSS-registered parcels valued under €150:
- €3.00 per distinct HS tariff heading (EU harmonised handling charge)
- Replaces previous customs clearance processing
- Multiple items in same HS heading = single €3 charge
- An Post processes and collects on behalf of Irish Revenue

CUSTOMS HOLD NOTIFICATION
An Post sends customs hold notification via:
- SMS to recipient mobile number
- Email to recipient email address
- Physical customs card through letterbox
Subject line patterns: "Customs charge due", "An Post customs notification", "Parcel held at customs"
From addresses: customs@anpost.com, notifications@anpost.ie, no-reply@anpost.ie

PAYMENT PORTAL
URL: https://www.anpost.com/Post-Parcels/Tracking/Customs-Charges
User enters: tracking number, customs reference number
Payment methods: credit card, debit card, PayPal
Payment deadline: typically 16 days from notification
Late payment: parcel returned to sender after deadline

TRACKING NUMBER FORMATS
An Post tracking: RR followed by 8 digits and IE (e.g. RR123456789IE)
USPS tracked: 22 digit numeric
China Post tracked: RR/RA/EV followed by 8 digits and CN

VAT RATE APPLIED BY AN POST
An Post applies 23% Irish VAT on top of declared value plus customs duty.

PARCEL VALUE THRESHOLDS
Under €22: no customs (de minimis — removed July 2021, now all values subject to VAT)
€22-€150: VAT only (if IOSS not registered); from July 2026 also €3/heading charge
Over €150: customs duty + VAT (IOSS irrelevant)
Over €700: formal customs entry required, additional agent fees may apply
