DHL EXPRESS — CUSTOMS AND DUTY FEES (EU IMPORT)
Source: DHL Express published rate cards (2025)

CUSTOMS CLEARANCE FEES (DHL Express)
DHL charges customs brokerage on behalf of the recipient.

Disbursement fee (advancement of customs duties):
- 2.5% of total customs duties and taxes advanced
- Minimum fee: €12.50
- Maximum fee: €50.00

Import clearance fee:
- EU imports: €16.00 flat fee per shipment (standard)
- Express: included in shipping cost if pre-paid by sender

Combined typical charge for a €100 parcel (non-IOSS, 12% duty):
- Customs duty: €12.00
- VAT 23%: €25.76
- DHL advancement fee: €12.50 (minimum, as 2.5% of €37.76 = €0.94, below minimum)
- Total at door: ~€50.26

Combined typical charge for a €500 parcel (12% duty):
- Customs duty: €60.00
- VAT 23%: €128.80
- DHL advancement fee: €4.72 (2.5% of €188.80)
- Total at door: ~€193.52

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION FROM DHL
DHL sends customs notification via:
- Email from: dhl-customs@dhl.com, customs-notification@dhl.com, no-reply@dhl.com
- Subject patterns: "Customs duty payment required", "Your DHL shipment requires payment", "Customs clearance required"
- SMS to recipient

DHL PAYMENT PORTAL
URL: https://www.dhl.com/ie-en/home/tracking/payment.html
Requires: waybill number, last name or postcode
Payment: credit/debit card, PayPal
Deadline: 5 business days from notification (parcel held at depot)

DHL TRACKING NUMBER FORMAT
DHL Express: 10 digit numeric starting with 1 or JD followed by digits
DHL eCommerce: GM followed by 16 digits

IOSS HANDLING
DHL accepts IOSS declarations on shipments under €150.
If IOSS number is valid: no customs charge at door.
If IOSS number invalid or missing: charges as non-IOSS.
DHL spot checks IOSS numbers against EU central database.
