FEDEX — CUSTOMS AND BROKERAGE FEES (EU IMPORT)
Source: FedEx published ancillary charges (2025)

CUSTOMS BROKERAGE FEES
FedEx charges a customs brokerage fee for clearing imports through customs.

FedEx International Priority / Economy:
- Disbursement fee: 2% of duties and taxes advanced
- Minimum: €15.00
- Maximum: €75.00

Informal entry (goods under €1,000):
- FedEx standard brokerage: €15.00 flat
- Plus duties and taxes

Formal entry (goods over €1,000):
- Brokerage: €25.00 + 1.5% of customs value
- Plus duties and taxes

CUSTOMS NOTIFICATION FROM FEDEX
Email from: fedex@fedex.com, customs@fedex.com, noreply@fedex.com
Subject patterns: "Customs clearance required", "Action required: FedEx shipment", "Duties and taxes due"
FedEx typically attempts delivery and leaves door tag if duties outstanding.

FEDEX PAYMENT PORTAL
URL: https://www.fedex.com/en-ie/tracking.html (enter tracking → pay duties)
Also: FedEx Delivery Manager app
Requires: tracking number, delivery address postcode
Payment: credit/debit card
Deadline: 7 days before parcel is returned

FEDEX TRACKING NUMBER FORMAT
FedEx: 12 digit numeric (e.g. 123456789012)
FedEx Ground: 15 or 22 digit numeric
FedEx SmartPost: 20 digit starting with 92

IOSS HANDLING
FedEx accepts IOSS on qualifying shipments under €150.
FedEx validates IOSS numbers at clearance.
Rejected IOSS (validation failure): treated as non-IOSS, full charges applied.
