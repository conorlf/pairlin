BTI Decision — Sportswear / Athletic Tops
Product: Men's training top made of 100% polyester knitted fabric, designed for sports use. Features moisture-wicking technology, raglan sleeves, mesh panels.
Classification: 6112.19.00 (Tracksuits, swimwear and other swimwear: of man-made fibres)
Duty rate: 12%
Reasoning: Although described as a training top, the construction (moisture-wicking, mesh panels) and intended use (sports activity) classify it under chapter 61 sportswear subheading rather than general T-shirts (6109). Sports design characteristics determine classification over generic description.

BTI Decision — Yoga/Gym Leggings (Women's)
Product: Women's leggings made of 80% nylon, 20% elastane, ankle length, worn during yoga and gym exercise. Four-way stretch fabric.
Classification: 6104.63.00 (Women's trousers, bib and brace overalls of synthetic fibres)
Duty rate: 12%
Reasoning: Leggings classified as trousers under chapter 61. Elastane content does not change classification. Sports end-use not determinative for lower chapter headings.

BTI Decision — Oversized Hoodie / Sweatshirt
Product: Unisex hoodie, 70% cotton 30% polyester fleece, kangaroo pocket, drawstring hood, ribbed cuffs.
Classification: 6110.20.10 (Jerseys, pullovers, sweatshirts, of cotton)
Duty rate: 12%
Reasoning: Classified by predominant fibre (cotton). Hood and pocket features do not affect heading. "Oversized" aesthetic does not create separate classification.

BTI Decision — Puffer Jacket / Padded Outerwear
Product: Women's puffer jacket, woven outer shell (100% polyester), padded with synthetic filling, designed for outdoor cold weather use.
Classification: 6201.93.00 (Men's or boys' anoraks, windcheaters, woven, of man-made fibres)
Alternative: 6202 for women's
Duty rate: 12%
Reasoning: Outerwear with filling classified under 6201/6202 based on gender. Padding material does not affect heading.

BTI Decision — Running Trainers / Sneakers
Product: Running shoe with rubber/plastics outer sole and textile upper (mesh), marketed as athletic footwear.
Classification: 6404.11.00 (Footwear with outer soles of rubber/plastics, uppers of textile, sports footwear)
Duty rate: 17%
Reasoning: Sports footwear subheading 6404.11 applies where shoe is designed for and marketed as athletic use. Mesh upper (textile) combined with rubber sole determines heading.

BTI Decision — Foam/EVA Slides / Sandals
Product: Unisex foam sandal (slide style), single-piece EVA construction, no textile components.
Classification: 6402.99.31 (Other footwear with outer soles and uppers of rubber or plastics)
Duty rate: 17%
Reasoning: Entire construction of rubber/plastics including both sole and upper. Value below EUR 7.50 per pair threshold triggers lower subheading rate in some cases but general rate is 17%.

BTI Decision — BB Cream / Tinted Moisturiser
Product: BB cream with SPF, marketed as both skincare and makeup. Provides coverage while moisturising. Ingredients include titanium dioxide (pigment), hyaluronic acid, SPF 30 filters.
Classification: 3304.99.00 (Beauty or make-up preparations not elsewhere specified)
Duty rate: 6.5%
Reasoning: Despite skincare claims, presence of pigments/colorants classifies product as makeup preparation under 3304 rather than skincare under 3307. The predominant function (coverage/colour) determines classification.

BTI Decision — Lip Gloss / Lip Oil
Product: Clear tinted lip product with shimmer, ingredients include oils, wax, mica pigments, vitamin E. Marketed as "lip gloss" or "lip oil".
Classification: 3304.10.00 (Lip make-up preparations)
Duty rate: 6.5%
Reasoning: Lip products containing any colouring matter or lustre classified under 3304.10 regardless of oil content or "natural" marketing. Mica/shimmer constitutes colouring.

BTI Decision — Perfume / Eau de Toilette
Product: Women's fragrance, 50ml, 30% fragrance concentrate in alcohol, marketed as "Eau de Parfum".
Classification: 3303.00.10 (Perfumes and toilet waters)
Duty rate: 0% (suspended)
Reasoning: Perfumes benefit from autonomous suspension of duty under EU common customs tariff. Zero duty applies regardless of origin.

BTI Decision — Hair Extensions / Wigs
Product: Human hair extensions, clip-in style, 100% Remy human hair.
Classification: 6703.00.00 (Human hair, dressed, thinned, bleached or otherwise worked)
Duty rate: 0%
Reasoning: Human hair products classified under 6703 at zero duty. Not classified under clothing or accessories headings.

BTI Decision — True Wireless Earbuds (TWS)
Product: Wireless earbuds, Bluetooth 5.0, includes charging case, active noise cancellation, touch controls.
Classification: 8518.30.95 (Headphones and earphones, whether or not combined with a microphone)
Duty rate: 0%
Reasoning: TWS earbuds with ANC classified under 8518 regardless of charging case. Zero duty under ITA (Information Technology Agreement).

BTI Decision — Smart Watch with Health Features
Product: Smartwatch with GPS, heart rate monitor, blood oxygen sensor, 7-day battery, iOS/Android compatible.
Classification: 8517.62.00 (Machines for the reception and conversion of voice, images or other data, including switching and routing apparatus)
Alternative: 9102.12.00 (Watches with mechanical display only, with automatic winding)
Duty rate: 0% (8517 classification, ITA applies)
Reasoning: EU Classification Committee concluded smartwatches primarily function as connected devices rather than traditional timepieces. 8517 classification attracts zero duty.

BTI Decision — Power Bank / Portable Battery
Product: Portable charger, 20000mAh, USB-A and USB-C outputs, fast charging, LED indicator.
Classification: 8507.60.00 (Lithium-ion accumulators)
Duty rate: 0% (ITA)
Reasoning: Power banks classified as accumulator (battery) under 8507. ITA zero duty applies.

BTI Decision — Phone Case (Silicone/Rubber)
Product: Silicone protective case for smartphone, flexible, includes cutouts for ports and camera.
Classification: 3926.90.97 (Other articles of plastics: other)
Duty rate: 6.5%
Reasoning: Silicone phone cases classified under plastics chapter 39. Not classified as part of telephone (8517) as it lacks functional purpose for communication.

BTI Decision — Crossbody / Shoulder Bag (PU Leather)
Product: Women's crossbody bag, outer surface PU (polyurethane coated textile), magnetic clasp, chain strap.
Classification: 4202.22.90 (Handbags whether or not with shoulder strap, with outer surface of sheeting of plastics or textile materials: other)
Duty rate: 3.7%
Reasoning: PU-coated textile outer surface classified under 4202. "Vegan leather" or PU materials classified as plastics sheeting. Chain strap does not affect classification.

BTI Decision — Canvas Tote Bag
Product: Reusable shopping bag, 100% cotton canvas, short handles, screen-printed design.
Classification: 4202.92.15 (Other bags and containers, with outer surface of textile materials: of cotton)
Duty rate: 3.7%
Reasoning: Tote bags classified under 4202 regardless of intended use as shopping bag. Cotton outer surface determines subheading.

BTI Decision — Hair Claw Clip / Jaw Clip
Product: Plastic hair claw clip, acetate material, various sizes.
Classification: 9615.11.00 (Combs, hair-slides and the like, of hard rubber or plastics)
Duty rate: 2.7%
Reasoning: Hair clips classified under 9615. Acetate/plastic material classifies under subheading 9615.11.

SPECIAL MEASURES AND SUSPENSIONS — IRELAND (IE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

AUTONOMOUS DUTY SUSPENSIONS (zero duty regardless of origin):
- Perfumes and toilet waters (3303): 0% suspended
- Pharmaceutical active ingredients (many 2900s/3000s): 0% suspended
- Aircraft parts: 0% suspended
- IT equipment covered by ITA: 0% suspended

ANTI-DUMPING MEASURES (additional duties on top of MFN):
- Chinese e-bikes (8714.10): Additional 48.5% anti-dumping duty
- Chinese solar panels (8541.40): Variable additional duty
- Certain Chinese steel: Additional duties apply

TRADE PREFERENCES — goods from these origins attract reduced rates:
- Products of Least Developed Countries (Everything But Arms): 0%
- Countries with EU FTA (Japan, Canada, South Korea, Vietnam, etc.): reduced rates
- Note: China (CN) — no general preference, full MFN rates apply
- UK (GB) — EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement: 0% for goods meeting rules of origin
- US (US) — no FTA, full MFN rates apply (as of 2026)

IOSS REFORM — FROM 1 JULY 2026:
Each distinct HS tariff heading in a single shipment under €150 attracts a €3 customs duty charge.
This replaces previous zero-duty treatment for IOSS sub-€150 goods.
Full customs duty + VAT continues to apply to all goods over €150 regardless of IOSS status.
